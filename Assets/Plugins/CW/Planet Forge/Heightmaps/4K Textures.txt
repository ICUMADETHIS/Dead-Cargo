To make this asset faster to upload and download, I've resized these heightmap textures to 2K resolution.

If you want the original 4K textures, then you can download them from here: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EqMPTYj7SoEHtU7m1Q_wtn5KuY6I8iP4

NOTE: The terrains are built from multiple layers of detail, so switching to 4K textures massively increases your build size, and only delivers a minor improvement in terrain detail, so I recommend sticking to 2K, even 1K is usually good enough.
